Mood Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The short version

Everything you write in Mood Tracker is stored on your device and nowhere else. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that holds your entries. I cannot read what you write, because it is never sent to me.

The app makes one kind of network request, and it never contains your journal. Details are in Network connections below.

What the app stores

Mood Tracker keeps two things in a database file inside the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your check-in entries — every answer you have recorded, with the date and time.
  • Your form — the questions you have chosen to answer, and how they are configured.
  • Your reminder settings — whether reminders are on, and the times you picked.

That is the whole of it. The app does not read your contacts, location, photos, health data, microphone, or camera, and does not ask for permission to do so.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no email address, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No third-party trackers or marketing tools of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your information, because none of it reaches me.

Network connections

When the app starts, it checks whether a newer version of its software is available. That request goes to Expo's update service (u.expo.dev), operated by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.).

Like any request over the internet, it necessarily reveals your device's IP address to that service, along with the app version, the platform, and the device model. It never contains your entries, your form, or anything you have written. Expo's handling of that request is covered by Expo's privacy policy.

If you would rather the app made no network requests at all, turning on Airplane Mode does not affect anything else: the app works fully offline, including reminders.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled by your device itself. They do not involve a push notification server, and no push token is created or sent anywhere. They work with no network connection. If you deny notification permission, the rest of the app is unaffected.

Device backups

Your journal is stored in the app's documents folder, which means iOS includes it in your device backups. If you have iCloud Backup switched on, a copy of your entries is part of that backup and is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not mine. The same applies to an encrypted backup made to your own computer.

You can exclude the app from iCloud Backup in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Backups.

Backups you create yourself

The app can export your journal to a file so you do not lose it if your phone is lost. The app writes that file and hands it to the iOS share sheet; you choose where it goes. It might be saved to the Files app, sent to yourself, or copied into a cloud service.

Once the file leaves the app it is an ordinary readable file, and it contains every entry you have written. Whatever you send it to is covered by that service's privacy policy, not this one. The app is not told where you sent it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a single entry from its detail screen.
  • Erase everything from Settings › Privacy & data › Erase everything.
  • Deleting the app removes its database along with it.

Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for me to delete on your behalf and no deletion request to make. Deleted entries cannot be recovered unless you exported a backup first.

Children

Mood Tracker is not directed at children, and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Not a medical service

Mood Tracker is a personal journal. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please contact your local emergency services. In the United States and Canada you can call or text 988 to reach a crisis counsellor.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects this policy, I will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
